# lexiarchive.com

# The two thank-you pages are deliberately NOT disallowed here. They are kept out
# of the index with `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` (netlify.toml), and a crawler has to be
# allowed to fetch a page before it can read that header. Blocking them here would
# hide the noindex and leave the URLs indexable by address alone -- the opposite of
# what the header is for.

# AI crawlers are allowed on purpose, and the choice is worth stating rather than
# leaving to the default.
#
# A legal professional in Morocco increasingly asks an assistant "what software
# archives my case files offline in Arabic?" instead of typing it into a search
# box. Being absent from that answer is indistinguishable from not existing. The
# whole site is public marketing copy about our own product -- there is no client
# data here and nothing to protect -- so the only thing a refusal would buy is
# invisibility in the channel this audience is moving to.
#
# Named individually rather than covered by the wildcard below, because a policy
# that matters should be legible instead of inferred. Two kinds are listed: those
# that gather training data (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended,
# CCBot, meta-externalagent) and those that fetch a page live to answer a question
# being asked right now (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, PerplexityBot).
# The second kind is the one that sends real visitors.
#
# To reverse this, change a group's `Allow: /` to `Disallow: /`. It takes effect on
# the next crawl and costs nothing else.
